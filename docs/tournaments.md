# Tournaments managed by the club — match-padel-api

Introduced by card #77 (`[Admin F4]`, API half). Code: `src/domains/tournaments/` — `fixtures.core.ts` (the four fixture
generators, pure), `bracket.core.ts` (results, bracket advancement, standings, pure), `tournament.core.ts` (registration,
payment confirmation, publish/cancel/start and results with their collaborators injected), `tournament.service.ts`
(wiring and views), `tournament-admin.*` (club panel routes), `tournament.router.ts`/`tournament.controller.ts` (player
routes). Scope reference: `match-padel-web/docs/club-platform-scope.md`, section 5.

> **Status: the registration payment is written from the Mercado Pago documentation and NOT verified against the sandbox**
> (card #73). Everything else was checked with property tests of the generators and fake-database flow checks (see the PR).

## Who does what

Only club staff manage tournaments: **owner and administrator** of the owning club (and platform superadmins). Reception
has no access. Players only list, follow, register and withdraw. A tournament of another club is 404 in the panel.
`POST /tournaments` and `POST /tournaments/:id/start` (the old player-side routes) are closed: 403
`TOURNAMENTS_MANAGED_BY_CLUBS` for anyone who is not staff of the club, 410 `TOURNAMENT_MANAGEMENT_MOVED`
(`details.endpoint`) for staff.

## Lifecycle

```
draft --publish--> open --start--> in_progress --last result--> completed
  \                  \                 \
   +----cancel--------+-----cancel------+--> cancelled   (cancel from draft / open / in_progress)
```

| Transition | Endpoint | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| create | `POST /club-admin/:clubId/tournaments` | club `approved` and active (409 `CLUB_NOT_APPROVED`), `start_date` in the future. Born `draft` |
| edit | `PATCH .../:id` | only `draft`/`open` (409 `INVALID_STATUS`). Structural fields (`format`, `max_teams`/`max_players`, `entry_fee`, `min_elo`, `max_elo`) only while there is no registration at all, paid or not (409 `TOURNAMENT_HAS_REGISTRATIONS`) |
| publish | `POST .../:id/publish` | `draft` only; club approved and active; dates in the future (409 `INVALID_DATES`); `entry_fee > 0` needs a club that can collect: 409 `CLUB_PAYMENTS_NOT_ENABLED` (`resolveSeller` / connected account check of card #75) |
| start | `POST .../:id/start` | `open` only; frees unpaid registrations; needs the minimum of confirmed teams (409 `NOT_ENOUGH_TEAMS`); generates the fixture; seeds the teams |
| result | `PATCH .../:id/matches/:matchId/result` | `in_progress` (or `completed`, for a correction) |
| complete | automatic | when the last match is resolved |
| cancel | `POST .../:id/cancel` | refunds every approved registration at 100%, notifies every registered player |

Minimum teams to start: round robin 2, single elimination 2, double elimination 3, americano 2 registrations (4 players).
Maximum 64 registrations (128 americano players).

### Sizing: teams and americano

A registration is always a pair (`player1_id` + `player2_id`, `tournament_teams`). `max_teams` = registrations. Americano is
played by individuals, so it is sized with `max_players` (even, 4..128) on create/update and stored as
`max_teams = max_players / 2`; both fields are in the responses (`max_players` is `null` outside americano). The entry fee
is **per registration** (one payment by the player who registers, for the pair) in every format.

### Level limits

`min_elo` / `max_elo` (nullable, inclusive) are checked for **both** players at registration (409 `LEVEL_NOT_ALLOWED`,
`details: { player_id, elo, min_elo, max_elo }`). `category` is free text (max 60).

## Fixtures

Generated by `fixtures.core.ts` from the confirmed registrations, best seed first. Seed = combined ELO of the two players
(`seeding: 'elo'`, default) or registration order (`'registration'`); ties by registration time, then id. The seed is stored
in `tournament_teams.seed`. Rows are persisted to `tournament_matches` in one insert; `bracket` of the tournament keeps
`{ format, seeding, generated_at, teams, matches, seed_order }`.

`tournament_matches` columns used: `stage` (`group` | `main` | `winners` | `losers` | `grand_final` | `americano`), `round`
(1-based), `position` (0-based inside stage and round, unique), `team1_id`/`team2_id`, `next_match_id` + `next_match_slot`
(where the winner goes), `loser_next_match_id` + `loser_next_match_slot` (double elimination), `team1_players` /
`team2_players` (americano), `status`: `pending` (teams not decided), `ready` (both known), `completed`, `bye`
(auto-resolved, one side never exists), `void` (nobody can ever play it).

### Round robin

Circle method. N teams (N even) play N-1 rounds of N/2 matches; with N odd a phantom is added and the team that draws it
rests that round (no row is stored for a rest): N rounds of (N-1)/2. Every pair meets exactly once. Sides alternate.

### Single elimination

Bracket padded to the next power of two S with the standard seeding order (`1 v S`, `2 v S-1`, ... so seeds 1 and 2 only meet
in the final). The `S - N` best seeds get a **bye**: their round 1 opponent slot is empty and the resolver advances them
(`status: 'bye'`). There is no third-place match: the two semifinal losers share placement 3.

### Double elimination

Winners bracket (as above, stage `winners`), losers bracket (stage `losers`) and **one grand final** (stage `grand_final`).
With S = 2^k: the losers bracket has 2(k-1) rounds; round 2i-1 pairs the survivors of the previous losers round (round 1:
the losers of winners round 1) and round 2i receives the losers of winners round i+1 (reversed order, to avoid immediate
rematches) against them. The winners champion and the losers champion meet once.
**Reset rule: there is NO bracket reset.** The grand final is a single match, and whoever wins it is the champion even when
it is the team coming from the losers bracket (documented simplification). Byes of round 1 cascade: a loser that never
existed makes the losers match a bye / void. A tournament of N teams plays 2N-2 matches (2N-1 when the losers champion
wins the final, because one extra loss is possible). Minimum 3 teams.

### Americano

Individual players (both players of every confirmed registration), circle method over N players (a phantom when N is odd).
Over N'-1 rounds (N' = N rounded up to even) **every player partners every other player exactly once**; the pairs of a round
are matched two against two (4 players per court, the pair order rotates each round so opponents vary). Minimum 4 players.
A player paired with the phantom rests; when the number of real pairs of a round is odd one pair sits out, chosen by a
deterministic beam search that minimizes the squared number of rests per player. Result: with N a multiple of 4 everyone
plays every round; otherwise the matches played per player differ by at most 2 (checked for N = 4..20). The number of
rounds is the full rotation by default; `rules.americano_rounds` (create/update) or `americano_rounds` in the start body
generate fewer.

## Results

`PATCH /club-admin/:clubId/tournaments/:id/matches/:matchId/result`, body
`{ score_team1: number[], score_team2: number[], winner?: 'team1' | 'team2' }`.

- Padel: games per set, 1..5 sets of equal length, e.g. `[6,4,7]` v `[3,6,5]`. Winner = more sets, then more games.
- A tie is refused (400 `WINNER_REQUIRED`) unless `winner` says who goes through; a `winner` that contradicts the score is 400
  `WINNER_MISMATCH`. Americano: one entry per side (the points of the side); a **draw is valid** (no winner).
- The winner moves into `next_match_id`/slot; in double elimination the loser drops into `loser_next_match_id`; byes advance
  automatically; the tournament completes when no match is left unresolved.
- **Corrections**: the same endpoint on an already played match. A score-only change is always allowed. A change of winner is
  allowed while no match fed by it has been played with the teams that came from it; otherwise 409 `RESULT_LOCKED`
  (`details.match_ids`). The bracket is recomputed from the recorded results, so a corrected winner replaces the old one
  downstream (including losers-bracket drops and byes).
- 409 `MATCH_NOT_READY` when its teams are not decided yet; 409 `INVALID_STATUS` when the tournament has not started or was
  cancelled. Every result is audited (`tournament.result_recorded`, before/after) and the players of the match are notified.
- No locking between two simultaneous results of different matches of the same bracket: the last write wins (rare, the panel
  has a single operator per club; target — not implemented yet: optimistic check).

## Standings

`GET /club-admin/:clubId/tournaments/:id/standings`. Before the fixture exists: `{ type: 'none', rows: [] }`.

**Round robin** (`type: 'round_robin'`): 3 points per win, 0 per loss. Ties on points are broken, in order, by head-to-head
points among the tied teams only (mini league), head-to-head set difference, head-to-head game difference, overall set
difference, overall game difference, games won, seed (lower first), team id. Positions are strictly ordered 1..N.

**Elimination** (`type: 'elimination'`): final placement as the bracket resolves. A team is out when it loses a match that has
no loser link (any match of single elimination; the losers bracket or the grand final of double elimination). Placement =
`1 + teams still alive + teams that lasted longer`, teams out in the same round share it (8 teams: champion 1, finalist 2,
semifinal losers 3, quarterfinal losers 5). `placement` is `null` while a team is alive. `status`: `champion` | `eliminated`
| `active`.

**Americano** (`type: 'americano'`): individual table. Points scored by the player's side in every match, then wins, then
point difference, then player id. Resting is part of the format, so totals are not normalized by matches played (`played`
is in the row).

## Registration payment (unverified against the sandbox)

`POST /tournaments/:id/teams` (the player pays for the pair):

1. Checks: tournament public (approved, active club; not a draft) and `open`; registration deadline; partner exists and is not
   the caller (400 `INVALID_PARTNER`); level limits; neither player already registered; capacity (confirmed teams only).
2. `entry_fee = 0`: the team is `confirmed` at once and the partner is notified.
3. `entry_fee > 0`: `assertClubCanCharge` first (409 `CLUB_PAYMENTS_NOT_ENABLED`, nothing is created), then the team is
   inserted `pending_payment` with `expires_at = now + 30 minutes` and a `payments` row (`tournament_id`,
   `tournament_team_id`, `reservation_id = null`) plus a Checkout Pro preference are created through the same charging
   abstraction as reservations (`resolveSeller`: the club's token and `marketplace_fee` for a `club_account` club, platform
   token for `platform_account`). **The amount is the tournament's `entry_fee` read by the server, never a client value.** The
   notification URL carries `?club_id=` as for every club charge.
4. Webhook (`external_reference = payments.id`, signature, seller and amount checks unchanged): an `approved` payment with a
   `tournament_id` runs the confirmation (`createConfirmationCore`):
   - team `pending_payment` and the tournament `open` and a place free: team `confirmed`, `paid_at` set, both players notified;
   - already confirmed with the same payment: no-op (idempotent); a different payment for a confirmed team: refunded in full;
   - the registration expired / was withdrawn, the tournament is no longer `open` (started, cancelled) or it filled up
     meanwhile: **the payment is refunded in full**; a refund that cannot be completed is left `refund_status = 'pending'`
     with its reason and the club's owners are notified (same mechanism as card #76).
   Only confirmed registrations count against `max_teams`; two simultaneous payments for the last place are resolved at
   confirmation time (the loser is refunded).
5. A rejected/cancelled notification changes nothing in the registration (Checkout Pro lets the buyer retry with the same
   preference); the unpaid registration releases its place by itself.

**TTL**: 30 minutes (`REGISTRATION_TTL_MINUTES`). Released by the job `expire-tournament-registrations` (every 5 minutes, same
pattern as `expire-unpaid-reservations`), lazily by the next registration of the same tournament, and by `start`. Releasing
cancels the pending checkout (`payments.status = 'cancelled'`); a late payment of it is refunded.

**Withdraw** (`DELETE /tournaments/:id/teams/me`): only while the tournament is `open` (409 `TOURNAMENT_ALREADY_STARTED` after
the start, 409 `TOURNAMENT_CANCELLED`). A confirmed paid registration is refunded by the club's refund policy
(`docs/refund-policy.md`; the cutoff is measured against the tournament's `start_date`: 100% at least `hours_before_start`
before it, `late_refund_percent` inside the window, 0 after); the response carries `refund` (`PlayerRefundResult`, `null`
when nothing was paid). Either player of the team may withdraw. The registration is removed.

**Cancel** (club, any time before it completes): status first (no new registration, late payments come back), then every
approved unsettled payment refunded at **100%** (club-initiated, never the player policy), pending checkouts cancelled,
every player of every registration notified (`tournament_update`, `data.kind = 'tournament_cancelled'`, `refunded`).

### Unverified assumptions

Same list as the charging flow (`docs/mercadopago.md`): `marketplace_fee` on the preference, the seller's token creating a
preference, `notification_url` with a query string, partial and full refunds with the club's token. Additionally: that
Checkout Pro notifies a second approved payment of the same preference with the same `external_reference` (handled as a
duplicate), and that a payment can still be approved after its preference was used for a rejected attempt.

## Visibility

- Public (`GET /tournaments`, `GET /tournaments/:id`): not a draft, club `approved` and `is_active`. Only `confirmed` teams.
- RLS (`docs/permissions.md`): same split for direct reads; members of the club and platform superadmins read every
  tournament, team and match of the club; the players of a registration read it whatever its status.
